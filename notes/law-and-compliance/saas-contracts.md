  - title: Service Level Agreement
    author: Common Paper
    url: "https://commonpaper.com/standards/service-level-agreement/"
    agents: "https://github.com/CommonPaper/SLA"
    type: framework
    year: 2024
    note: "A standard SLA, version 2.0 under CC BY 4.0, that adds to the Cloud Service Agreement. It defines how uptime and support response time are measured, what is excluded, and how service credits are claimed. Missing the uptime target in two of three consecutive months lets the customer terminate with a prorated refund."
  - title: "Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/915 on standard contractual clauses between controllers and processors"
    author: European Commission
    url: "https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2021/915/oj/eng"
    agents: "https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/2b85eb9d-c72b-11eb-a925-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1"
    type: law
    year: 2021
    note: "The Commission's optional clauses for a data processing agreement, which meet the contract terms of Article 28 GDPR. Annexes list the parties, the processing, the security measures and the sub-processors, approved one by one or under a general authorisation. They do not cover transfers outside the EU, which need the separate transfer clauses."
  - title: "Opinion 22/2024 on certain obligations following from the reliance on processor(s) and sub-processor(s)"

    type: guide
    year: 2024
    note: "The EDPB's opinion, requested by the Danish authority, on what a customer must know and check about its processor's chain of sub-processors. The customer should always have the identity of every sub-processor, and the processor should keep that information current. It also covers checking guarantees according to risk and transfers between sub-processors."
  - title: "Commission Recommendation (EU) 2026/2394 on non-binding model contractual terms on data access and use and non-binding standard contractual clauses for cloud computing contracts"

    note: "Non-binding clauses for cloud contracts, adopted under Article 41 of the Data Act. Annexes VI to XI cover switching and exit, termination, security and business continuity, non-dispersion, liability and non-amendment, and Annex XII defines their terms. They are meant to be inserted into an existing service agreement."
