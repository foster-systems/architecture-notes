title: EU Data Act
description: "The EU regulation on access to data from connected products and on switching between cloud services, SaaS included."
why: "Its switching rules cover SaaS as well as infrastructure: contracts must let customers leave, data must be exportable through open interfaces, and switching charges end on 12 January 2027. A SaaS product tied to a connected device also falls under its data access rules."
