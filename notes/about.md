I am Przemek Łuniewski, a solution architect and AI engineering lead at [Foster Systems](https://foster.systems). I deliver software of any kind, AI or not, with a fleet of AI agents. These notes are what I read, use and think about while doing that.

## What you find here

- **Topics**: one page per topic, grouped in sections. Each has my take, when it fits and when it doesn't, and 5–7 sources I picked, each with a note on why it is worth your time.
- **Posts**: a short comment on each change to the notes, what was added, removed or changed, and why. Each post links to the change itself on GitHub.

Topics are kept current, not dated. When something better appears, the list changes and a post says why.

## How sources are chosen

I link, I don't copy. Every source is one I assessed; an agent may suggest one, but only my approval adds it. Notes are in my own words. Quotes are rare and short.

## For agents

Read [llms.txt](../llms.txt) for the index. Append `.md` to any topic URL for its Markdown, or read the [repo](https://github.com/foster-systems/architecture-notes) directly. Front matter holds the summary, why it matters, and the sources.

## Suggest a source

Open an [issue](https://github.com/foster-systems/architecture-notes/issues) with the link and one line on why.
