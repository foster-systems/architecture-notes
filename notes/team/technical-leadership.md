why: "At a small company one person often sets technical direction, manages people and still ships code. Somewhere between about 6 and 30 engineers those jobs stop fitting in one person, and the CTO has to choose what to keep and what to hire for."

    url: "https://www.startuplessonslearned.com/2008/09/what-does-startup-cto-actually-do.html"
    type: article

He lists six skills, from platform choice and finding the 80/20 to growing Technical Leads and owning the development methodology.   - title: "The CTO Journey at a Small Startup"

    url: "https://zapier.com/blog/startup-cto/"

    year: 2017
    note: "Zapier's co-founder and CTO maps the role by team size: 1 to 2 engineers, 2 to 6, 6 to 12, then 12 and up. A table shows the share of time spent coding at each stage. Past 12 engineers he describes a fork between a VP of Engineering path and a CTO path, and how to choose."
  - title: "Differences between VP Engineering and CTO"
    author: Hugo Lassiège
    url: "https://eventuallymaking.io/p/differences-between-vp-engineering-and-cto"
    type: article
    year: 2021
    note: "Malt's founding CTO on hiring a VP of Engineering, with about 30 people in the product team and no engineering manager yet. He gives the team sizes at which he thinks each layer becomes necessary. He sets out how Malt split the work and the alternatives to the hire, such as a Principal Engineer."
  - title: "#define CTO"
    author: Greg Brockman
    url: "https://blog.gregbrockman.com/figuring-out-the-cto-role-at-stripe"
    type: article
    year: 2014
    note: "Brockman's account of going from early engineer to CTO at Stripe and hiring a VP Engineering at about 25 engineers to take over one-on-ones and recruiting. It covers delegating without micromanaging, losing visibility into the organisation, and returning to code. It reports what about 20 other CTOs and VPs of Engineering said about the role."
  - title: "From Founder to CTO"
