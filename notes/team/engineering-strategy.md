    type: guide

    note: "Larson's book on engineering strategy, free to read online. It adapts Richard Rumelt's Good Strategy, Bad Strategy to engineering and sets out five steps: explore, diagnose, refine, set policy and run operations. More than half its case studies come from his work at Uber, Stripe and Calm, such as Uber's service migration and Stripe's API deprecation. The rest use unnamed or hypothetical companies, on topics such as monolith decomposition and LLM adoption."
