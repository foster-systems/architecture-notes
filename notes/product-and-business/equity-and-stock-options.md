  - title: "How to Split Equity Among Co-Founders"
    author: Michael Seibel
    url: "https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/splitting-equity-among-founders"
    type: article
    year: 2015
    note: "Michael Seibel, then a Y Combinator partner and a co-founder of Justin.tv and Socialcam, argues for equal equity splits among co-founders. He lists the usual reasons for unequal splits and answers that most of the work of building a company lies ahead, and that investors read the split as a sign of how the CEO values the co-founders. It is the advice YC usually gives."
  - title: "The Equity Equation"
    author: Paul Graham
    url: "https://paulgraham.com/equity.html"
    type: article
    year: 2007
    note: "A short essay with one test for any trade of stock, for money, an employee or a deal: giving up a fraction n is worth it if it makes the company worth more than 1/(1 − n) times as much. It works the test through examples such as a Y Combinator investment and a venture round."
  - title: "Five Things You Should Know About Founder Stock Vesting"
    author: WilmerHale
    url: "https://launch.wilmerhale.com/research/blog/five-things-about-founder-stock-vesting"
    type: article
    year: 2015
    note: "A US law firm's advice on vesting founders' restricted stock: the standard four-year schedule with a one-year cliff, the company's right to buy back unvested shares, and when vesting starts. It recommends an 83(b) election within 30 days and double-trigger acceleration on a sale over single-trigger. It warns that unusual terms invite investors to reset vesting at the first institutional round."
  - title: "What is a Section 83(b) Election and Why Should You File One?"
    author: Matthew Bartus, Cooley GO
    url: "https://www.cooleygo.com/section-83b-election-file-one/"
    type: guide
    year: 2025
    note: "A partner at the law firm Cooley explains the US Section 83(b) election, which taxes stock that is subject to vesting when it is granted and not as it vests. Two worked examples show the tax difference, and the election must be filed within 30 days of the grant. It covers restricted stock such as founders' shares; options follow other rules."
  - title: "Employee Equity: How Much?"
    author: Fred Wilson
    url: "https://avc.com/2010/11/employee-equity-how-much/"
    type: article
    year: 2010
    note: "A venture investor's method for sizing employee grants once a company has a core team: a multiplier on base salary by seniority gives a dollar value of equity, which the company's current valuation turns into shares. The first few key hires get percentage points set by judgement. A note added to the post says the 2010 multipliers have moved a lot since and that using them would put grants below market."
  - title: "Rewarding Talent: The Founder's Guide to Stock Options"
    author: Index Ventures
    url: "https://www.indexventures.com/rewarding-talent/"
    type: guide
    year: 2018
    note: "A venture firm's free book on employee stock options for European startups, in its second edition. It draws on cap tables from 73 portfolio companies, more than 4,000 option grants, founder interviews and a survey, and covers how ownership changes over rounds, how much to grant, vesting, leaver terms and country tax rules. The country rules were last updated between 2018 and early 2021 and should be checked against current law."
