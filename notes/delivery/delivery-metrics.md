  - title: "DORA's software delivery performance metrics"
    author: Nathen Harvey (DORA)
    url: "https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics/"
    type: guide
    year: 2026
    note: "DORA's definition of its five metrics, in two groups. Throughput covers change lead time, deployment frequency and failed deployment recovery time. Instability covers change fail rate and deployment rework rate."
  - title: State of AI-assisted Software Development 2025
    author: DORA
    url: "https://dora.dev/research/2025/dora-report/"
    type: paper
    year: 2025
    note: "The 2025 edition of DORA's annual research report, from a program run by Google Cloud. Its main finding is that AI amplifies an organization's existing strengths and weaknesses."
  - title: "The SPACE of Developer Productivity: There's more to it than you think"
    author: "Nicole Forsgren, Margaret-Anne Storey, Chandra Maddila, Tom Zimmermann, Brian Houck, Jenna Butler"
    url: "https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-space-of-developer-productivity-theres-more-to-it-than-you-think/"
    type: paper
    year: 2021
    note: "Microsoft Research's page for the ACM Queue paper on the SPACE framework. It states that productivity cannot be measured by a single metric or dimension. The page has the abstract and a link to the article."
  - title: "DevEx: What Actually Drives Productivity"
    author: "Abi Noda, Margaret-Anne Storey, Nicole Forsgren, Michaela Greiler"
    url: "https://getdx.com/research/devex-what-actually-drives-productivity/"
    type: paper
    year: 2023
    note: "DX's archive entry for the paper, with the abstract and a link to the full text at ACM Queue. The paper gives a framework for understanding developer experience and a way to measure it from developer feedback and system data."
  - title: Measuring Engineering Productivity
    author: Ciera Jaspan
    url: "https://abseil.io/resources/swe-book/html/ch07.html"
    type: guide
    year: 2020
    note: "A chapter of Software Engineering at Google. It covers when a measurement is worth making, the Goals/Signals/Metrics framework for tying each metric to a goal, and checking metrics against qualitative data."
  - title: The Worst Programmer I Know
    author: Dan North
    url: "https://dannorth.net/blog/the-worst-programmer/"
    type: article
    year: 2023
    note: "A short story about a developer whose individual metrics read zero because he spent his time pairing. It argues for measuring team accountability and business impact."
  - title: Cannot Measure Productivity
    author: Martin Fowler
    url: "https://martinfowler.com/bliki/CannotMeasureProductivity.html"
    type: article
    year: 2003
    note: "A short argument that software productivity cannot be reliably measured because output cannot. It covers why lines of code and function points fail as measures."
